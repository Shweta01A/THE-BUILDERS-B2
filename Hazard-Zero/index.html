Hello!

Welcome to Hazard Zero Safety Training

Before entering the warehouse, please read this short briefing.

  • You will explore a realistic warehouse and identify workplace hazards.
  • Correctly identifying hazards increases your score.
  • Incorrect decisions deduct points and raise your risk level.
  • Ignoring a hazard can increase the risk level. Three unsafe decisions end the training.
  • Follow safe workplace procedures at all times.
  • The training has a 5 minute time limit.
  • Your final result shows your performance.
