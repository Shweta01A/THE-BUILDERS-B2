Our Product

HAZARD ZERO

Interactive 360° Warehouse Safety Training

Overview

Training that puts people inside the decision.

HAZARD ZERO places trainees inside a realistic 360° warehouse environment. As they move through the space, they encounter the same categories of hazard found on a real floor — spills, exposed cabling, blocked exits, fire risk — and have to identify and respond to each one. Every choice gets immediate feedback, so the lesson is learned in the moment, not read afterwards in a report.

How It Works

Four steps, every scenario.
