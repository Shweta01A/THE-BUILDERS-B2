About The Builders

Technology With a Purpose.

Who we are

The Builders is a workplace-safety technology studio. We started from a simple observation: most safety training is written to be completed, not remembered. So we build tools that put people inside a decision instead of in front of a slideshow — starting with HAZARD ZERO, our interactive warehouse safety trainer.

M

Mission

Give every warehouse worker the judgement to catch a hazard before it becomes an incident.

V

Vision

A workplace where safety training is something people engage with, not something they sit through.

V

Values

Practical over polished, evidence over assumption, and the floor worker's experience over the org chart's.

Our Approach

Built from the floor up, not the office down.

  • We design training scenarios from real hazard categories — spills, exposed cabling, blocked exits, fire risk — not generic quiz questions.
  • Every scenario gives immediate feedback, so the lesson lands at the moment the decision is made.
  • We build for supervisors too: HAZARD ZERO's dashboard shows where teams are actually struggling, not just who finished the course.
